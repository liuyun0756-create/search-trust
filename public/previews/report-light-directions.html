02 / What we would do

Three actions to discuss, not a generic checklist.

  1. Validate demand before building

    Confirm service scope, customer need and the existing page inventory. Then approve a page or a documented no-build decision.

  2. Align public business information

    Resolve differences between the website and public business profile.

  3. Clarify the contact path

    Make the next step clear on the core service pages in scope.

The findings above support a first conversation. They do not prove ranking impact, inquiries, or revenue. Private performance data can test the direction later.
